The problem

Patients check carefully before they book. Gaps cost you trust.

A parent with a feverish child searches late in the evening. They look for a clinic open now, the doctor's name, whether insurance is accepted and a number to call. If your profile has missing hours, no specialty details or unanswered reviews, they move on to the next clinic. Healthcare marketing also has rules, so every word on your profile and website needs care.

Typical Dubai searches: dentist near me open now, dermatologist in Jumeirah, pediatrician Dubai accepts insurance, clinic open on Friday, Arabic speaking doctor Dubai, physiotherapy Business Bay.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can we post patient testimonials?

Rules differ by regulator and by case, so check the healthcare authority's advertising guidelines and get written consent first. We help write the text, but you stay responsible for compliance.

Can you promise more patients?

No. We improve how easily patients can find and contact you, then report calls, direction requests and appointment requests.

Do we need separate pages for each doctor or treatment?

Usually yes for your main specialties. A dedicated page answers specific searches better than one general page.

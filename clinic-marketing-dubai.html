The problem: patients check carefully before they book, and gaps cost you trust

A parent with a feverish child searches late in the evening. They look for a clinic open now, the doctor's name, whether insurance is accepted and a number to call. If your profile has missing hours, no specialty details or unanswered reviews, they move on to the next clinic. Healthcare marketing also has rules, so every word on your profile and website needs care.

Before they call, patients usually check:

  • Is the clinic open now, including evenings and Fridays?
  • Is this the right specialty and the right doctor?
  • Does the clinic speak my language, and does it accept my insurance?
  • Where is it, and where do I park or enter?
  • Do the reviews look recent, and does the clinic reply politely?

Typical Dubai searches: dentist near me open now, dermatologist in Jumeirah, pediatrician Dubai accepts insurance, clinic open on Friday, Arabic speaking doctor Dubai, physiotherapy Business Bay.

Our approach: rules and privacy first

Healthcare advertising in Dubai is regulated, for example by the Dubai Health Authority (DHA), and requirements can change. We keep wording plain and accurate and never promise results or share patient details. You remain responsible for confirming claims and any approvals with your regulator before publishing.

Frequently asked questions

Can we post patient testimonials?

Rules on testimonials in healthcare advertising can be strict, and patient privacy comes first. Check your regulator's current rules and get the patient's written consent before sharing any story. Reviews that patients write on Google can be answered politely without mentioning any medical detail.

Can you promise more patients?

No. Nobody can honestly promise a number of patients or a position on Google. We improve how clearly and accurately your clinic appears, and we track calls, direction requests and enquiries so you can see what changes.

Do we need separate pages for each doctor or treatment?

Usually one page for each main service is best, with each page covering one topic. Doctor pages help when you can show qualifications, but confirm what you may publish with your regulator.

How long does it take to see results?

Fixes to your Google profile can show within weeks. Website pages usually take longer, often a few months, to rise in search.

Do you work in Arabic and English?

Yes. We prepare profile and website content in both languages, so patients can find you in the language they search in.

What do you need from us?

Your clinic details, licensed services, opening hours, doctors' details you are allowed to publish, and manager access to your Google Business Profile.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Should we show prices online?

Yes, starting prices work well. They attract the right clients and save time answering the same question.

Is Instagram enough for bookings?

It builds interest, but many people check Google Maps before booking. You need both, linked to one clear booking route.

How do we ask for reviews without being pushy?

Send a short thank-you message right after the appointment, with a direct link. Most happy clients say yes when it takes one tap.

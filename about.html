03 / Why Togba Local Hub

We help local businesses compete online.

Big-agency thinking with a neighbourhood focus, built for businesses with a real front door.

Local businesses
are our business.

We started this agency because too many great local businesses — the neighbourhood restaurant, the family-run salon, the independent gym — get outworked online by bigger competitors with bigger budgets, even when their product and service are better.

Our focus is narrow on purpose: we only work with local, in-person businesses. That means every strategy we build is designed around what actually matters to you — more calls, more bookings, and more people walking through your door.

Alfonso Togba Salomon
Founder, Togba Local Hub

The founder's perspective

From mixing drinks behind a bar in Monrovia, Liberia, to mixing strategy behind a screen in Dubai — I've built my career on the same principle: pay attention, know who you're serving, and never let someone feel like just another number.

After years in hospitality, I trained hands-on in SEO, Google Ads, Meta Ads, and analytics, earned my Google Digital Marketing certification, and founded Togba Local Hub to bring that same personal attention to local businesses — whether they're in Liberia, the UAE, or anywhere in between.

Alfonso Togba Salomon
Founder, Togba Local Hub

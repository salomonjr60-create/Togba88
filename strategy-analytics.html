Data Analytics & Continuous Optimization

Measure what works, replace guesswork with evidence, and improve your marketing over time.

Analytics helps you understand website and campaign performance so you can make decisions based on evidence. Every other strategy becomes more valuable when you can see which channels produce meaningful results.

Why it matters

Without measurement, you cannot know which channels are producing results, and your budget may be wasted on activity that only appears to work.

Where to start

Set up basic website analytics and conversion tracking before spending seriously on marketing. Review the data regularly and improve one clear part of the customer journey at a time.

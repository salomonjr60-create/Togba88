Frequently asked questions

Should we offer a free trial?

That is your decision. A trial lowers the barrier for new people, and our job is to make it easy to find and request.

When should we run ads?

Often before busy sign-up periods, such as the new year or after a school holiday. We start small and measure cost per trial request.

How do we get more member reviews?

Ask after a class, when people feel good, with a direct link sent by WhatsApp.

How long until we see more customers from Google?

Profile fixes can show within weeks, but ranking and review growth take longer. We can't promise a position, so we track calls, direction requests and messages each month.

Do we still need delivery apps?

That is your choice. A strong profile and direct ordering links help you keep more orders and fees for yourself, and the two can work together.

Should we reply to bad reviews?

Yes. A calm, specific reply shows future guests how you handle problems. We help you write replies.

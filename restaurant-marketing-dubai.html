Frequently asked questions

Restaurant marketing FAQs

How can my Dubai restaurant appear more often in Google Maps?

Keep your Google Business Profile accurate and complete, choose appropriate categories, maintain current hours and menus, upload authentic photos, and make your location and dining offering clear on your website. Visibility also depends on Google's local ranking systems and competition; no position can be guaranteed.

Should a restaurant invest in local SEO or Google Ads?

Local SEO supports ongoing discovery for relevant searches, while Google Ads can provide paid visibility for selected keywords and locations. The best mix depends on your budget, competition, and whether you need short-term promotion or sustained organic growth.

Can you help with reservations and takeaway enquiries?

We can review the customer journey and recommend clearer booking, calling, ordering, and contact links. Actual booking or ordering integrations depend on the systems your restaurant uses.

Do small cafés and independent restaurants need a dedicated website?

A well-maintained Google Business Profile is useful, but a website gives you more control over menus, stories, events, private dining information, and conversion paths. It also creates opportunities to rank for relevant organic searches.

How long does restaurant SEO take to show results?

Timing varies by your website's current condition, local competition, content quality, and Google's crawling and ranking decisions. Some technical fixes can be completed quickly, but organic visibility often takes weeks or months to develop.

What happens during a free restaurant marketing audit?

We review the publicly visible elements of your restaurant's online presence and identify practical opportunities related to local visibility, website experience, and customer enquiries. The scope of any follow-up work is discussed separately.

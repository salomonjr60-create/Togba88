Pay-Per-Click Advertising (Google Ads)

Reach high-intent customers immediately and pay only when someone clicks your ad.

Google Ads places your business at the top of search results when customers are actively looking for what you offer. Unlike SEO, campaigns can start producing visibility quickly.

Why it matters

Paid search can reach a customer at the exact moment they are ready to buy, call, book, or request a quote.

Where to start

Set a focused budget, target a small group of high-intent keywords, and measure calls, forms, and bookings before scaling the campaigns that work.
